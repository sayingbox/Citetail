Generative Engine Optimization
Get built for generative AI, not just search rankings

GEO: earn the citation before the answer is even written.

Generative Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring and writing content so that large language models — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity — are more likely to pull from it when composing an answer. CiteTail tracks whether it's actually working.

Get Free Report See the ranking factors
Definition

GEO optimizes for the model, not the crawler.

Traditional SEO is built around how search crawlers index and rank pages. GEO is built around how generative models retrieve, weigh, and synthesize information into an answer. That means clearer topical structure, direct and quotable phrasing, stronger source signals, and content that's easy for a model to extract and attribute — rather than content built purely to rank in ten blue links.

What CiteTail tracks

The GEO factors that decide whether you get cited.

Each of these gets scored per page, so you know exactly what's holding a piece of content back.

01

Content structure & clarity

Clear headings, direct answers near the top, and scannable sections make it easier for a model to extract a clean quote.

02

Source authority signals

Author bios, citations, original data, and outbound references that establish your page as a credible source.

03

Direct-answer formatting

Definitions, comparisons, and step lists phrased the way a model would want to quote them back.

04

Structured data & schema

FAQ, HowTo, and Article schema that give models an unambiguous, machine-readable version of your content.

05

Freshness & updates

Regularly updated pages signal currency — important for models trained to prefer recent, accurate information.

06

Topical depth

Comprehensive coverage of a subject, rather than thin pages, gives a model more reason to treat you as the source.
