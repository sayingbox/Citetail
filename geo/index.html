GEO: Generative Engine Optimization.

Generative Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring and writing content so that large language models — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity — are more likely to pull from it when composing an answer. Citetail tracks whether it's actually working.

Get Free Report See the ranking factors
Definition

GEO optimizes for the model, not the crawler.

Traditional SEO is built around how search crawlers index and rank pages. GEO is built around how generative models retrieve, weigh, and synthesize information into an answer. That means clearer topical structure, direct and quotable phrasing, stronger source signals, and content that's easy for a model to extract and attribute — rather than content built purely to rank in ten blue links.

Comprehensive coverage of a subject, rather than thin pages, gives a model more reason to treat you as the source.

Know the difference

SEO vs. GEO vs. AEO.

Three different optimization targets, three different outcomes. Here's how they actually compare.

GEO and AEO overlap heavily — both care about clarity, structure, and getting quoted verbatim. Citetail tracks all three, but our core focus is GEO: getting your content cited inside AI-generated answers, not just ranked or read aloud.

FAQ

Common questions about GEO.

Everything you need to know before you start optimizing for AI answer engines.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring content so it gets surfaced and cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, rather than optimizing purely for traditional search engine rankings.
SEO optimizes for ranking in a list of blue links that a user clicks through, while GEO optimizes for being quoted or cited directly inside an AI-generated answer, where the user may never visit your site at all. GEO places more weight on factual clarity, structured data, and quotable statements than on backlinks or keyword density.
The six commonly cited GEO ranking factors are: relevance to the query, authoritativeness of the source, structured and specific content, freshness of information, citation frequency across the web, and technical crawlability including structured data markup.
Improve your GEO score by publishing clear, fact-based content with specific numbers and comparisons, adding structured data like FAQPage and Organization schema, earning citations from authoritative third-party sources, and regularly updating content to stay current.
You can track GEO performance using tools like Citetail, which monitor where your content is being cited across AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and AI Overviews, and benchmark your citation share against competitors over time.
