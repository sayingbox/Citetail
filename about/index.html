Same shift, two names

GEO and AEO, from two directions.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) structures and writes content so models draw on it when composing an answer.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) makes sure your brand appears in the direct answer itself, not a list of links.

Whichever term your team uses, Citetail tracks the same outcome: are you the one AI chooses to cite?
