AEO: win the answer box, the AI reply, and the voice response.

Answer Engine Optimization is about making sure your brand shows up in the direct answer itself — featured snippets, AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity replies, even voice assistants — instead of a ranked list of links underneath. CiteTail tells you where you're winning and losing that spot.

Definition

AEO optimizes for the answer, not the click.

Where SEO measures rank and GEO measures model citations, AEO is focused on one specific surface: the direct-answer format — featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, AI Overviews, voice assistant replies, and chat answers from ChatGPT or Perplexity. Winning that spot means your content is the answer, whether or not anyone ever clicks through.

What CiteTail tracks

The AEO signals that decide who gets the answer spot.

Each of these gets scored per page, so you know exactly what's keeping you out of the answer.

Q&A formatting

The same fact stated the same way across your site — conflicting answers hurt eligibility across every engine.
