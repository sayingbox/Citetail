Why this needs its own tracking

ChatGPT doesn't rank you. It decides for the shopper.

A search engine hands someone ten blue links and lets them choose. ChatGPT hands them one synthesized answer. If your brand isn't in that answer, you weren't a candidate — even if you'd have won the click.

1
One answer, not ten links
Citetail logs the full answer text, not just whether your domain appears — including how you're framed next to competitors.
2
Position inside the answer
Named first, named in passing, or left out entirely — each gets tracked as its own outcome, not a single "mentioned" flag.
3
It changes without warning
The same question can get a different answer week to week. Citetail reruns your query set daily so drift shows up before a quarter closes.
Citation — a brand or source ChatGPT names directly inside its answer text.
Query set — the real buying questions Citetail runs for your category on a schedule.
Citation position — where in the answer you're named: first, secondary, or absent.

Questions

Before you start tracking.

How is this different from ChatGPT's own "Browse" citations?
Browse citations only show up on queries where ChatGPT chooses to search the live web. Citetail also tracks the far larger set of answers ChatGPT composes from what it already knows about your category, which is where most brand mentions actually happen.
How often does the query set refresh?
Daily on Growth and Pro plans, weekly on Starter. You can also add or retire individual queries at any time as your category shifts.
Can I track competitors, not just my own brand?
Yes — every tracked query shows every brand cited in that answer, so competitor citations show up automatically alongside yours, no extra setup needed.
Do you support Custom GPTs?
Yes, for the Custom GPTs relevant to your category. Tell us which ones matter to your buyers and we'll add them to your tracked surface.
