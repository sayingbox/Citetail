Why this needs its own tracking

Claude reasons through an answer, then names its sources.

Claude tends to explain its thinking and, when it searches the web, shows its work with explicit citations. That's good news for brands with strong content — but only if you can see which answers you're actually named in, and which ones a competitor won instead.

Questions

Before you start tracking.

How is this different from tracking ChatGPT?
Same underlying idea, but the query set and parsing are tuned to how Claude actually answers and cites — including when it browses versus when it's answering from what it already knows. If you track both, you'll see where the two genuinely diverge.
Does this cover Claude Projects, or just claude.ai chat?
We track standard claude.ai conversations by default. If your buyers are likely to encounter your category inside a specific Claude Project or workspace, tell us and we'll add it to your tracked surface.
How often does the query set refresh?
Daily on Growth and Pro plans, weekly on Starter. You can also add or retire individual queries at any time as your category shifts.
Can I track competitors, not just my own brand?
Yes — every tracked query shows every brand cited in that answer, so competitor citations show up automatically alongside yours, no extra setup needed.
