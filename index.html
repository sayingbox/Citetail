Same shift, two names

GEO and AEO are the same idea, from two directions.

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization

Optimizing for the model

Structuring and writing content so generative AI models are more likely to draw on it when composing an answer — the content side of the shift.

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

Optimizing for the answer

Making sure your brand shows up in the direct answer itself — on ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews — rather than a ranked list of links.

Whichever term your team uses, CiteTail tracks the same outcome: whether your content is the one an AI engine chooses to cite, quote, or link back to.
