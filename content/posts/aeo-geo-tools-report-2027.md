AI-powered search is changing how people discover brands, products and information. As platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity become part of the search journey, businesses are increasingly looking for ways to measure their visibility in AI-generated answers.

Our **AEO & GEO Tools Report 2027** examines six platforms that help businesses monitor and understand their presence across AI search experiences.

### Tools Covered in This Report

1. Citetail
2. AthenaHQ
3. Rankscale
4. Rankability
5. SE Ranking (SE Visible)
6. Ahrefs Brand Radar

### What Does the Report Cover?

The report compares these platforms across key areas, including:

- AI search visibility and brand mentions
- Citation and source tracking
- Competitor monitoring
- Reporting and analytics
- Content optimization capabilities
- Platform features and use cases

The comparison focuses on publicly documented capabilities, without assigning promotional rankings or declaring a universal winner.

### Final Thoughts

As AI search continues to evolve, understanding brand visibility across answer engines will become an important part of digital marketing measurement. Choosing the right platform depends on business requirements, reporting needs and the AI search experiences a company wants to monitor.
