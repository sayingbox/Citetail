## About This Report

The AEO & GEO Industry Report 2027 provides an overview of the changing search and digital discovery landscape.

It examines how AI-powered platforms are influencing information discovery, how organisations are approaching brand visibility in generative responses, and how the industry is developing new approaches to measuring AI search performance.

The report brings together industry research, platform developments, market observations and selected software platforms to provide a broader understanding of this emerging field.

## What the Report Covers

- **AEO and GEO Landscape:** Understanding the concepts, differences and relationship with traditional SEO.
- **AI Search Developments:** How AI-powered search and conversational platforms are changing information discovery.
- **Industry Trends:** Key developments influencing the AEO and GEO ecosystem.
- **Brand Visibility and Citations:** How organisations assess their presence in AI-generated responses.
- **Measurement and Analytics:** Emerging metrics for tracking AI visibility and performance.
- **Software Landscape:** An overview of selected AEO, GEO and AI visibility platforms.
- **Challenges and Future Outlook:** Industry limitations, opportunities and expected developments in 2027.
