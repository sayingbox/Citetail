# How Razorpay Improved AI Reliability & Brand Consistency with Citetail

*Razorpay used Citetail's beta to identify and fix gaps in how AI engines represented their brand — improving their AI Reliability score from 60% to 72% and Brand Consistency score from 70% to 86%.*

## The Problem

As more people research payment and fintech providers through AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini instead of traditional search, how a brand is represented in those answers matters as much as traditional SEO once did. For a company like Razorpay, that means two things need to hold up: what AI engines say about them needs to be accurate and positive, and it needs to be consistent everywhere — their own site, review platforms, directories, and third-party listings.

Going into their Citetail beta usage, Razorpay's scores reflected two gaps:

- **AI Reliability: 60%** — meaning a meaningful share of AI-generated answers about Razorpay were incomplete, outdated, or not aligned with how the brand wanted to be represented.
- **Brand Consistency: 70%** — indicating some mismatch between how Razorpay was described across reputed listings, directories, and review sites, which can confuse both AI models and prospective customers.

## What They Did

Razorpay used two core parts of the Citetail toolkit to close these gaps:

**Sentiment Analysis + AI SEO Agent** Citetail's Sentiment Analysis surfaced where AI-generated mentions of Razorpay skewed neutral or unclear rather than clearly positive, and flagged specific patterns behind the lower AI Reliability score. The AI SEO Agent turned those findings into prioritized, actionable fixes — rather than leaving the team to guess at what to prioritize — which Razorpay's team worked through systematically.

**Brand Consistency Checks** Razorpay also used Citetail to check whether reputed listings, directories, and review sites were describing the brand consistently — the same core facts, positioning, and details across every third-party source AI models might pull from. Inconsistent information across those sources is a common reason AI engines give incomplete or conflicting answers about a brand, and closing those gaps was central to the consistency score improvement.

## The Results


| Metric | Before | After |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| AI Reliability Score | 60% | 72% |
| Brand Consistency Score | 70% | 86% |


By acting on Citetail's Sentiment Analysis and AI SEO Agent recommendations, and cleaning up inconsistencies flagged across listings and review platforms, Razorpay moved both scores meaningfully in a relatively short window — closing gaps that would otherwise have kept showing up as incomplete or inconsistent AI-generated answers about the brand.
